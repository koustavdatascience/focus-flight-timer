# Meridian public-name audit

**Assessment date:** 14 August 2026  
**Purpose:** Initial public web and app-usage screen for the proposed FocusFlight rebrand to **Meridian**. This is not a trademark clearance or legal opinion.

## Verified finding

| Source | Current usage | Relevance |
|---|---|---|
| [Meridian — Plan & Focus App on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/meridian-plan-focus/id6761740690) | An active, free iPhone/iPad Productivity app, released by Dhinesh Thangamani / Quark Labs. Its listed features include day planning, goals, task lists, custom focus-timer profiles, and focus sessions. | **Direct category overlap.** The exact proposed name is already used for a consumer productivity/focus product. |

## Initial implication

The current finding is sufficient to pause any Meridian rebrand. Additional public search results also show widespread use of Meridian by unrelated software and commercial organizations, but the active focus/productivity application alone creates a material branding-confusion risk for this product category.

## Recommendation

Do not change product copy, SEO, OAuth branding, or domains to Meridian. Select a more distinctive candidate and repeat the public screen before taking rebrand action.
