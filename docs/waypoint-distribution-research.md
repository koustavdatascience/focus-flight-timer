# Waypoint Distribution Research Notes

## Product Hunt

Product Hunt’s posting guidance says a product can be posted by its maker using a personal account, and recommends a direct product URL, a square thumbnail, a short tagline, a small number of relevant topics, a gallery, clear pricing/status, and a first comment that starts a conversation. It also supports scheduled launches or saved drafts. Source: [How to post a product](https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/479557-how-to-post-a-product).

Its maker-forum guidance recommends authentic, useful participation: sharing work in public, asking for feedback, posting practical guides, making content searchable, publishing factual changelog updates, and giving launch recaps. Product forums notify product followers about threads, so they can form an owned update channel over time. Source: [Maker’s guide to Product Forums](https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/11432379-maker-s-guide-to-product-forums).

## Instagram

Instagram states that original content is encouraged for better reach and distribution. It advises creators to post content they made themselves or with approved partners, and distinguishes material edits from simple watermarking or stitched reposts. Source: [About original content on Instagram](https://help.instagram.com/1800814370401535/).

## Reddit

Reddit’s platform rules require users to follow individual community rules, participate authentically in communities they genuinely care about, and avoid spam or disruptive behavior. Promotion must therefore be treated as community-specific, with each subreddit’s rules and moderator guidance checked before any post is drafted or published. Source: [Reddit Rules](https://redditinc.com/policies/reddit-rules).

## Initial relevance signals

Search results surfaced productivity-focused communities including r/ProductivityApps, r/productivity, r/pomodoro, and r/getdisciplined. These are potential research targets, not automatic posting destinations; their current individual promotion policies must be reviewed before outreach.
