# Waypoint public-name audit

**Assessment date:** 14 August 2026  
**Purpose:** Initial public web and app-usage screen for the proposed FocusFlight rebrand to **Waypoint**. This is not a trademark clearance or legal opinion.

## Outcome

**Do not proceed with a Waypoint rebrand at this stage.** The word is heavily used across consumer products and established businesses, the exact `.com` domain is occupied, and a discontinued productivity product used the closely related name **Waypoint AI**.

## Verified findings

| Source | Current or historical usage | Relevance |
|---|---|---|
| [waypoint.com](https://waypoint.com/) | Active Waypoint Resource Group website for accounts-receivable management. | The exact primary domain is unavailable and already carries an established commercial brand. |
| [app-waypoint.com](https://app-waypoint.com/) | Waypoint AI, a discontinued productivity assistant for tasks, goals, and projects. | High category adjacency even though the service is no longer available; users may associate the name with productivity software. |

Public search results also surfaced current or recent use of Waypoint in navigation, trip-planning, and mobile app categories. Those results reinforce that the word is crowded in the same broad direction-and-journey space as FocusFlight.

## Recommendation

Choose a more distinctive coined or two-word brand before changing product copy, OAuth branding, metadata, or domains. A candidate should then receive a focused web/app/domain screen and, before launch, professional trademark advice for the intended countries and classes.
